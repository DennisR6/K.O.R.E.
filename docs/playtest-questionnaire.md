# Human Playtest Questionnaire

Complete this form privately with the tester immediately after Match 2. Read
each prompt as written. Use a 1-5 rating unless the question requests a free
response. Do not replace a rating with an observer opinion.

## Rating Scale

- `1`: strongly disagree
- `2`: disagree
- `3`: mixed or unsure
- `4`: agree
- `5`: strongly agree

## Questions

```text
session_id: <non-identifying ID>

1. The goal of the match was understandable. rating: <1-5>
   comment: <what made it clear or unclear>
2. I could tell which team was active. rating: <1-5>
   comment: <what helped or confused you>
3. Aiming and selecting a figure were understandable. rating: <1-5>
   comment: <what you expected to happen>
4. Power and the effect of drag distance were understandable. rating: <1-5>
   comment: <what you expected to happen>
5. The item phase and item choices were understandable. rating: <1-5>
   comment: <include "not applicable" only if the phase was unavailable>
6. Feedback after an action was immediate and clear. rating: <1-5>
   comment: <what happened and what was missing>
7. The camera or framing obstructed me. rating: <1-5>
   comment: <where the arena or feedback was obscured>
8. Something about the outcome or available actions felt unfair. yes/no
   details: <what happened, including whether it was expected>
9. The match was too short, too long, or about right. choice: <too short|too long|about right>
   details: <when the pacing changed>
10. I would voluntarily play another match. yes/no/unsure
    why: <brief reason>

most memorable moment: <brief answer>
most confusing moment: <brief answer>
one change that would help: <brief answer>
tester_quotes: <short verbatim quotes only>
```

Question 7 is intentionally phrased as an obstruction question: a `5` means
the camera was strongly obstructive and should be reviewed separately from the
positive camera-usability rating in the protocol. A missing answer is a data
quality issue, not a neutral score.
